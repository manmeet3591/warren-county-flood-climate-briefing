# Warren County Flood and Climate Briefing

A discussion briefing for Warren County, Kentucky, planning and floodplain management: what radar satellites can show after a flood, what a future climate assessment could tell planners, and ten project ideas for Warren County.

**Live page:** https://manmeet3591.github.io/warren-county-flood-climate-briefing/

This is a research prototype, not a forecast or warning. The April 2025 example uses NASA/JPL OPERA DSWx-S1 surface water maps (doi:10.5067/OPDSWS1-L3V1), which contain modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2025), USGS gage height for station 03287500 and National Weather Service flood categories.

Manmeet Singh and M. M. Nabi, Western Kentucky University.
